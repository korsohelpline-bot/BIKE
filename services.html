Replace and rebuild teeth

Dental Implants

A dental implant replaces a missing tooth root with a titanium post that fuses naturally with your jawbone, topped with a custom crown. It restores full chewing strength and a natural look, and with proper care can last a lifetime.

Full Mouth Implant

For extensive tooth loss, full mouth implants anchor an entire arch of replacement teeth to a small number of implant posts — giving you the strength, stability, and look of natural teeth, permanently.

Dental Bridges

A dental bridge fills the gap left by one or more missing teeth by anchoring a replacement tooth to the adjacent natural teeth or implants — restoring your bite and preventing teeth from shifting.

Dentures

Custom-fitted full or partial dentures replace missing teeth with a comfortable, natural-looking solution, restoring the ability to eat and speak with confidence.

Full Mouth Rehabilitation

Full mouth rehabilitation combines multiple treatments — from implants to crowns to bite correction — to rebuild every damaged or worn tooth, restoring both function and a complete, confident smile.

Straighten your teeth

Braces

Traditional metal or ceramic braces gradually guide teeth into proper alignment using gentle, continuous pressure. They remain one of the most effective solutions for correcting crowding, gaps, and bite issues at any age.

Clear Aligners

Clear aligners are removable, virtually invisible trays that straighten teeth step-by-step without metal wires. They're comfortable, easy to clean around, and fit seamlessly into your daily routine.

Pain relief, surgery and urgent care

When decay or infection reaches the inner pulp of a tooth, root canal therapy removes the damaged tissue, cleans the canal, and seals it — relieving pain and saving the natural tooth from extraction.
