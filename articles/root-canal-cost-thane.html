How Much Does a Root Canal Cost in Thane?

A clear breakdown of pricing, and what actually affects the final bill.

Root canal treatment (RCT) is one of the most misunderstood procedures in dentistry — mostly because pricing can vary widely between clinics, and it's rarely explained clearly upfront. Here's what actually goes into the cost.

What a Root Canal Actually Involves

A root canal removes infected or damaged pulp from inside a tooth, cleans and disinfects the canal, then seals it — usually followed by a crown to protect the tooth long-term. The procedure itself typically takes one to three sittings, depending on the tooth and severity of infection.

Factors That Affect the Cost

  • Which tooth is treated — molars have more canals than front teeth, making treatment more complex and time-consuming.
  • Severity of infection — a tooth with an abscess or significant decay may need additional visits or medication before the canal is sealed.
  • Type of crown chosen afterward — ceramic, zirconia, and metal-fused crowns are priced very differently.
  • Technology used — clinics using rotary endodontic systems and digital X-rays are often faster and more precise, which can affect pricing.
  • Number of sittings required — straightforward cases may finish in one visit; complicated ones need two or three.

Why Prices Vary So Much Between Clinics

A significantly cheaper quote often means older equipment, rushed procedures, or a crown made of lower-grade material. On the other hand, the most expensive clinic isn't automatically the best. Ask for a written treatment plan that separates the root canal procedure cost from the crown cost, so you know exactly what you're paying for at each stage.

Is It Worth Comparing Clinics on Price Alone?

Root canal treatment protects a natural tooth that would otherwise need extraction. A poorly done root canal can fail and require retreatment — which ends up costing more than getting it right the first time. Prioritize clinics that use rubber dam isolation, proper sterilization, and rotary instrumentation over ones offering the lowest possible price.
