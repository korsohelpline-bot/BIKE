Understanding Implants

What Is a Dental Implant?

A simple, honest explanation of how a dental implant replaces a missing tooth from the root up.

A dental implant is a small titanium post placed into the jawbone to act as an artificial tooth root. Over the following months, the bone naturally fuses with the titanium — a process called osseointegration — creating a stable, permanent foundation.

Once healing is confirmed, we attach an abutment and a custom-shaded crown on top, giving you a replacement tooth that looks, feels and bites just like your own — without touching the healthy teeth next to it.

Unlike a bridge or a removable denture, an implant actively preserves your jawbone, preventing the sunken, aged appearance that often follows long-term tooth loss.

How It Works

The Dental Implant Procedure, Step by Step

1

Consultation & 3D Scan

We assess your oral health, take a 3D scan to check bone density, and plan the exact implant position.

2

Implant Placement

The titanium implant is placed under local anaesthesia — most patients feel pressure rather than pain.

3

Osseointegration (Healing)

Over 3-6 months, the implant fuses naturally with your jawbone to form a permanent, stable base.

4

Abutment Placement

A small connector is attached to the implant once healing is confirmed at your follow-up visit.

5

Custom Crown Fitting

A crown, custom-shaded to match your smile, is fitted — completing your new, permanent tooth.

Is It Right for You

Who Is a Good Candidate?

  • Missing one, several, or all of your teeth in an arch
  • Have generally healthy gums and adequate jawbone density (or are open to a bone graft)
  • Wearing a loose or uncomfortable denture and want a fixed alternative
  • In good general health — conditions like uncontrolled diabetes or heavy smoking are reviewed during consultation
  • There is no upper age limit for dental implants
Common Questions

Dental Implants FAQ

Does getting a dental implant hurt?

The procedure is done under local anaesthesia, so most patients feel pressure rather than pain during placement. Mild soreness for a few days afterward is normal and managed easily with prescribed medication.

How long do dental implants last?

With good oral hygiene and regular check-ups, dental implants commonly last 15 to 25 years or longer, making them the most durable tooth replacement option available.

How much does a dental implant cost in Thane?

Cost depends on the number of implants, whether a bone graft is needed, and the crown material chosen. A precise quote is only possible after a consultation and 3D scan. See our cost guide for a full breakdown.

How long is the recovery after implant surgery?

Most patients return to normal activity within a few days. Full fusion of the implant with the jawbone (osseointegration) takes about three to six months before the final crown is fitted.

Am I too old for a dental implant?

There is no upper age limit for dental implants — what matters is bone density and overall health, both assessed during your consultation.

What is the difference between All-on-4 and a single tooth implant?

A single tooth implant replaces one missing tooth. All-on-4 anchors a full arch of 10-14 replacement teeth on just four implants, designed for patients missing most or all of their teeth.
