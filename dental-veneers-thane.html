Dental Veneers in Thane

Thin, custom shells for a flawless, natural-looking smile.

Quick answer

Dental veneers are thin, custom-made shells bonded to the front of your teeth to correct chips, gaps, discolouration or shape. They are a fast route to a more even, natural-looking smile. The Golden Tooth in Thane plans veneers carefully, often with digital smile designing, so the result suits your face.

About dental veneers

Veneers cover the visible surface of the tooth. They can transform the way teeth look in a few visits, and are often chosen when whitening alone is not enough. Because preparing the tooth usually involves removing a thin layer of enamel, your dentist first checks that veneers are the right choice for you.

Who it is for

  • Chipped or worn front teeth
  • Gaps between teeth
  • Stains that whitening cannot remove
  • Teeth with an uneven shape or size

Benefits

  • Corrects several concerns in one treatment
  • Natural look when well matched to your teeth
  • Ceramic veneers resist staining well
  • Planned in advance so you can preview the design

What happens during treatment

  1. Consultation and smile design
  2. Careful preparation of the teeth, usually involving a thin layer of enamel
  3. Impressions or scans to make your veneers
  4. Temporary veneers in some cases while yours are made
  5. Bonding of the final veneers, followed by a review visit

What to expect afterwards

Your teeth may feel slightly sensitive at first. Avoid biting very hard objects such as ice or pen caps, and tell your dentist if you grind your teeth, as a night guard may be advised. Brush, floss and attend regular cleanings to keep the edges healthy.

Frequently asked questions

How long do veneers last?

With good care many last for years. Longevity depends on the material, your bite and habits such as nail biting or grinding.

Are veneers reversible?

Preparing the tooth usually involves removing a thin layer of enamel, so veneers are generally considered permanent. Your dentist will explain this before you decide.

Do veneers stain?

Ceramic generally resists stains better than composite resin. Your dentist will advise on the best material for you.

Who should not get veneers?

People with untreated decay, gum disease or heavy teeth grinding may need those issues addressed first.
